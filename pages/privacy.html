Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Merge Nexa ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our Salesforce AppExchange application and this website.

1. Our Core Privacy Commitment

Merge Nexa is a 100% native Salesforce managed package. We do not transmit, store, or process your Salesforce data on any external servers. All duplicate detection and merging happens entirely within your Salesforce org. We have no access to your Salesforce records.

2. Information We Collect

When you contact us, request a demo, or use our website, we may collect:

  • Name and email address (from contact forms)
  • Company name and Salesforce Org ID (if provided voluntarily)
  • Browser type, IP address, and pages visited (via standard web analytics)
  • Communication records (emails and support tickets)

We do NOT collect any data from inside your Salesforce org. Your Salesforce records, contacts, accounts, and any data processed by the Merge Nexa managed package remain entirely within your Salesforce environment.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information collected through this website is used solely to:

  • Respond to support requests and inquiries
  • Send product updates you have opted into
  • Improve our website and documentation
  • Process AppExchange licensing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Salesforce AppExchange Package

The Merge Nexa managed package installed in your Salesforce org:

  • Runs entirely as Apex code inside your Salesforce org
  • Makes API calls only within your org (no external callouts)
  • Does not store data outside Salesforce
  • Respects all Salesforce sharing rules, profiles, and field-level security
  • Has been reviewed and approved by Salesforce's Security Review team

5. Cookies and Analytics

This website uses standard analytics tools (e.g., aggregated page view counting) to understand how visitors interact with our content. We use cookies for session management and analytics. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect some website functionality.

6. Data Retention

We retain contact information for as long as is necessary to provide support and fulfil our contractual obligations. You may request deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at privacy@mergenexa.io.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights including:

  • Access to personal data we hold about you
  • Correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion of your data
  • Objection to processing
  • Data portability

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@mergenexa.io.

8. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect the limited personal information we collect through this website. Communication with our support team is encrypted in transit.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised policy on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@mergenexa.io
  • Support form: Contact page
